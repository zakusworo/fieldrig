# Changelog

## 0.3.0 (13 November 2026)

First public release.

- Map and map library: DXF (with 3D TINs), Surpac STR, KML, CSV, GeoPDF and PDF drawings, offline MBTiles/PMTiles basemaps, cut & fill.
- Stakeout of lines, grids and design points; GPS tracks and boundary walks; map ruler.
- Compass and clinometer as graphical instruments, sun compass, GPS camera with address and QR code on the stamp.
- Field stations, stereonet with slope check, drillhole logging, sample QA/QC with chain of custody.
- Geophysics reading logs: magnetics, resistivity profiles, VES, other readings.
- Over 45 calculators for survey, geology, geophysics, mining, processing and safety, each with two worked examples.
- P2H pre-start checks and daily field reports (PDF).
- Demo data for training and presentations (Settings → Demo data).
