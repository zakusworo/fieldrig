# Changelog

## 0.3.3 (16 November 2026)

- New look for Home: a warm header with the date, time and your name, and every tool on a rounded tile with its own icon, so tools are easier to find at a glance.
- Rounder buttons and panels across the app; tool screens keep the dark title bar and ruler.
- New app icon: bolder hammer and pick over the open pit.
- Nothing changes in your data: install over 0.3.2 and everything stays.

## 0.3.2 (16 November 2026)

- Under the hood: Fieldrig is now built on a shared core with two sister apps (oil and gas, renewable energy), so fixes to the map, camera, tracks, checklists and calculators reach all of them.
- P2H shows what to do when there are no checks yet; clearer theme hint in Settings.
- Nothing changes in your data: install over 0.3.1 and everything stays.

## 0.3.1 (13 November 2026)

- How-to guide for every tool: tap **?** in a tool's title bar, or open **How to use Fieldrig** on Home. Also in [docs/TUTORIAL.md](docs/TUTORIAL.md).
- A new install follows the phone's light or dark theme, and asks for location after you sign in.

## 0.3.0 (13 November 2026)

First public release.

- Map and map library: DXF (with 3D TINs), Surpac STR, KML, CSV, GeoPDF and PDF drawings, offline MBTiles/PMTiles basemaps, cut & fill.
- Stakeout of lines, grids and design points; GPS tracks and boundary walks; map ruler.
- Compass and clinometer as graphical instruments, sun compass, GPS camera with address and QR code on the stamp.
- Field stations, stereonet with slope check, drillhole logging, sample QA/QC with chain of custody.
- Geophysics reading logs: magnetics, resistivity profiles, VES, other readings.
- Over 45 calculators for survey, geology, geophysics, mining, processing and safety, each with two worked examples.
- P2H pre-start checks and daily field reports (PDF).
- Demo data for training and presentations (Settings → Demo data).
